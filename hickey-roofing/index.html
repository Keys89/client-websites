Family-Owned Since 1985.

Hickey Roofing was founded in 1985 by Bill Hickey in Oshkosh. Today the business is run by Bill and his son Dan Hickey, who oversee every project that comes through their door to maintain quality standards.

Bill holds CertainTeed's Select ShingleMaster Tenured Member status — one of only a few hundred roofers in North America with the credential. We are members of the National Roofing Association and the Better Business Bureau, and have earned multiple "Best of Winnebago" and "Best of Oshkosh" awards from 2008 to 2023.
