Three Generations of Comfort.

RJ Heating and Air Conditioning was founded in 1976 by Jules Hopfensperger, who brought decades of HVAC know-how from his family's business in Marinette. Today it's led by his son Julius, alongside co-owner Erin, continuing three generations of hands-on service.

We tailor every comfort solution to the needs of the home, not a one-size-fits-all fix. Our CFC-certified technicians have been recognized among Milwaukee's best HVAC teams by Expertise.com in 2021, 2024, and 2026.
