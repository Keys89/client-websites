Family-Owned Since 1959.

West Allis Heating, Cooling, Plumbing, & Electrical has been a fixture of the greater Milwaukee area since 1959, when Floyd and Lois Brink founded the company. It has stayed family-owned ever since, now led by their grandson, Dan Eaton.

What started as a heating and cooling shop has grown into a full-service team handling heating, cooling, plumbing, and electrical work, backed by an A+ Better Business Bureau rating and technicians on call around the clock.
